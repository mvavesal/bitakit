# Code of conduct

BitaKit adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in the repositories, issues, pull requests, Discussions and
every other space where the project is represented.

In short: be respectful and kind, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback,
and make room for people of every background and level of experience. Harassment, personal
attacks and discriminatory language are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code of conduct, write to
[mva.vesal@gmail.com](mailto:mva.vesal@gmail.com). Reports are handled privately; only the people who need to act
on a report will see it.

Please include what happened, where (links if possible), when, and who was involved. You can
report anonymously, although that may limit what we can do.

## Addressing harm

The maintainers review every report and respond as described in the Contributor Covenant's
section on addressing and repairing harm: from a private conversation and a request to correct
the behaviour, to a temporary or permanent removal from the project's spaces. A maintainer
involved in a report takes no part in handling it.
