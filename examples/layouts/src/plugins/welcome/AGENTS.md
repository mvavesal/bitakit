# Welcome

An onboarding page at `/welcome` in the app's own `onboarding` layout family.

## Public contracts

`ui/pages/index.tsx` exports `layout = 'onboarding'`; the family comes from
`src/layouts/onboarding.tsx`. No services, Actions or settings.

## Constraints and checks

Open `/welcome`: no sidebar or header, the "Step 1 of 3" line from the layout.
